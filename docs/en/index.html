02 / ONE PROCESS, SIX STEPSNO BULK CLEANUP

Measure. Understand.
Change with purpose.

The goal is to remove unnecessary context while keeping your project moving. Finding that nothing needs to change is a good outcome, too.

  1. 01
    baseline

    Start with what is there.

    Version, stack, essential tasks, and an initial snapshot of your context.

    ⌁
  2. 02
    inventory

    Know what is loaded.

    Skills, plugins, MCPs, hooks, and memory, including their sources and scopes.

    ⊞
  3. 03
    analysis

    Separate useful from unnecessary.

    Consider actual use, observed cost, and dependencies. Rarely used does not mean useless.

    ≋
  4. 04
    one-change-at-a-time

    Change one thing at a time.

    One reversible recommendation, with its impact, scope, and a way back.

    ↗
  5. 05
    verify

    Measure again. Test the workflow.

    Compare the same metric and confirm that the useful capability still works.

    ✓
  6. 06
    final report

    Leave with evidence.

    Before and after, decisions, verified capabilities, and open issues in one report.

    ≡

05 / NO BLACK BOX

Worth knowing.

Will this reduce my tokens by 87.4%?+

Possibly YES, or even more. That percentage is the reduction in skills marked as never used in our anonymized case: from 389 to 49. Token savings need their own comparable measurements. The process aims to reduce unnecessary context without promising a universal result.

Does it work with Claude, Codex, and Antigravity?+

V1 is structured as a skills plugin for ChatGPT/Codex, and the environment it audits is Claude Code. People using Antigravity and other agents can explore and apply the principles; native support and audits of those environments have not been validated in this version.

Does the coach disable or delete anything on its own?+

No. It analyzes only the evidence you provide, suggests one reversible change at a time, and waits for you to apply it. There is no direct computer access or automatic execution of changes.

What do I need to share?+

Your version, stack, essential tasks, and outputs or screenshots of the requested diagnostics, such as Stats and /skills. Redact keys, tokens, and private data before sharing. The conversation remains subject to the host platform’s policies.Read the privacy policy (PT).

06 / NEXT CHAPTER V2 · PLANNING

Less manual collection.
You stay in control.

We want to make audits easier with an optional, read-only local companion. The same philosophy: minimal context, maximum utility.

01 / GUIDED READING

An inventory with context.

Identify settings, skills, and integrations, distinguishing global configuration from project-level configuration.

02 / EXPLICIT SCOPE

You choose the scope.

Consent and authorized paths before reading, with secrets redacted from shared evidence.

03 / BEFORE AND AFTER

Compare, then decide.

Comparable snapshots to evaluate one change at a time and check that useful workflows still work.

A product direction, with no release date yet. MCP or a local app will be chosen after access boundaries and data handling are validated. V2 is not included in V1 and does not plan to make automatic changes to your computer.
